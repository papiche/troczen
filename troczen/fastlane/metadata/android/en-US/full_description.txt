TrocZen creates, transfers, and redeems ẐEN vouchers — a local cooperative
currency — fully offline, for local markets and communities.

Key features:

* Offline-first: works without internet after the morning sync
* SSSS polynomial secret sharing (2-of-3) to split each voucher's private
  key across issuer, holder and network, preventing double-spending
* Atomic handshake: a double QR scan (Offer → ACK) with a random challenge
  and signature, so a voucher can never be redeemed twice
* Compact binary QR codes (v2, 240 bytes, fully offline)
* Collectible-card-style voucher display
* Circuit reveal: vouchers returned to the issuer publish an encrypted
  economic proof (NOSTR Kind 30304) instead of being silently destroyed
* Decentralized sync via the NOSTR protocol (kinds 0, 1, 3, 5, 7, 30303,
  30304, 30305, 30500+)
* NFC tap-to-transfer support

Cryptography: SSSS (2-of-3), AES-GCM, Schnorr/secp256k1 (BIP-340), Scrypt,
in-memory secure zeroisation.

Privacy: no tracking, no ads, no Google Play Services.
